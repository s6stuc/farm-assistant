After due consideration of covid and government guidelines, we opened for the first time since lockdown.

We've been up during lockdown usually less then 4 people at a time, to keep the bare essentials going and look after the chickens. As it was the first day in a while, we decided that we'd only have existing members on site to give us a chance to get back in the swing of things and also to ensure we knew what we were doing when new people arrived.

## Lots of jobs to do ##

{% include image.html src="2020/07/19/job-board.jpg" caption="Lots of jobs to do!" %}

## A couple of old swings ##

In true make-do fashion, on an almost zero budget project, we re-used a couple of frames from children's swings to as frames for netting to protect our precious veg from birds. The kids would much rather climb and swing from all the trees on the farm anyway.

{% include image.html src="2020/07/19/recycled-swing-frames.jpg" caption="We recycled some swings to make frames for netting to protect our veg" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/07/19/one-netted-area.jpeg" caption="New netted area is complete" %}

The usefulness of the swings didn't end there. Other parts of it were used to extend the legs on an old tub thingy to make it a more ergonomic height to use as a potting table.

{% include image.html src="2020/07/19/potting-table.jpeg" caption="A potting table with extended legs" %}
