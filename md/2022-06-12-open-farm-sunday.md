We had an amazing day. The weather was great. Vistor numbers were up. We raised more money than previous years.

[LEAF Open Farm Sunday](https://farmsunday.org/about-us) is a national event which sees farmers across the country open up their farms to visitors, enabling everyone to discover more about where our food comes from. LEAF OFS organises the whole things and supplies all it's participants with free resources thanks to it's [generous sponsors](https://farmsunday.org/about-us/sponsors).

Our Open Farm Sunday event had a real buzz about it this year, obviously due to COVID it's had to be cancelled two years in a row. Also in part due to lock-down we've had real growth in our membership and that brings renewed enthusiasm, and means we can do more.
