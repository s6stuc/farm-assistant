Roundhouses were the standard form of housing built in Britain from the Bronze Age through to the Iron Age.

This is our social space, to have meetings, hide from the rain, eat lunch and in the evenings sit around the fire and have a few drinks!

In memory of Dave Buckle, or “Mushroom Dave”.
