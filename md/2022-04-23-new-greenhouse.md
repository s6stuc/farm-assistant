## Is it a greenhouse though?

When you say greenhouse I think glass, well it doesn't have glass it has **poly**carbonate panels, and it's shape is more **tunnel** like than a traditional greenhouse, so is it a polytunnel or a greenhouse. I think we'll just call it the **big greenhouse**.
