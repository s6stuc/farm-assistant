Our 2nd October workday felt very autumnal although after the recent rainfall we were happy with a mostly dry day!

The first job of the day was to clean the chickens out.  We are trialling using coffee chaff as a bedding material which is a waste product from the coffee roasting industry.  We are grateful to [Forge Coffee Roasters](https://forgecoffeeroasters.co.uk/) for donating us some to try.  Our first impressions are good.  It is very light compared to our
usual bedding but has a slight coffee aroma which was still noticeable after several days.
{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/coffee-chaff.jpg" caption="Coffee chaff as bedding for chickens - thanks to Forge Coffee Roasters" %}

We also cleaned out the chicken run and hosed down the concrete base which we rebuilt this year to create a cleaner, drier area so the chickens are ready for the winter months.
{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/cleaning-chicken-outhouse.jpg" caption="Cleaning the chicken run" %}


The pigs had been suffering with the wet weather and although they love mud, they don't like it in their house so the pig arc was moved to a drier patch and refilled with straw which they seemed to appreciate.
{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/moving-pig-arc.jpg" caption="Moved the pig arc to s slightly less muddy area" %}

{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/pigs-get-sweetcorn-stalks.jpg" caption="They also enjoyed the left over sweetcorn stalks once we had harvested the sweetcorn." %}

We still managed to harvest a decent box with the last of the tomatoes and sweetcorn and some chard, leeks and peppers but the main work for the day was improving the animal areas.
{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/protect-poultry-from-foxes-and-badgers.jpg" caption="The poultry area was given a mesh 'skirt' to prevent foxes and badgers from digging under the fence" %}

{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/pig-loading-gate.jpg" caption="The pig load baying was completed to join the bottom pen ready for the pigs going" %}

{% include image.html src="2019/10/20/time-for-fire.jpg" caption="A bonfire too - all kids seemed to come to the farm armed with flint and steel strikers" %}

The day was completed with a members meeting in the roundhouse followed by a meal at the Wisewood Inn.
