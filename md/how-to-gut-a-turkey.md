{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/1.jpg" caption="Chop the head off the turkey leaving a long neck attached to the bird. This will ensure there is enough skin to cover the breast meat and a cavity to stuff (if you wish to)" %} 

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/2.jpg" caption="Turn turkey breast side down and slice along the back of the neck lengthways" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/3.jpg" caption="Unpeal the skin from the neck and use your fingers to free the windpipe and esophagus (spelling?!) from the neck. You should end up with a clean neck and a flap of skin that will eventually be the part you stuff" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/4.jpg" caption="Slice around the ankles of the bird. Do not cut too deep otherwise you slice the tendons. The aim is to pull the tendons out as they are inedible." %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/5.jpg" caption="Holding the leg in one hand, snap the ankle back and forth until it is free apart from the tendons then pull the foot removing as many tendons as possible. (it's not the end of the world if you have to cut them but whoever slices the meat needs to remove when cooked!)" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/6.jpg" caption="This is what the tendons look like once pulled. Repeat process with other foot!" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/7.jpg" caption="Locate the loose skin between the bottom of the ribs and the anus. Use a sharp knife to make a cut in a straight line between the two. Do not cut too deep as you do not want to pierce the intestines or gall bladder" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/8.jpg" caption="Push the knife (or your fingers) through the skin and fat and pull it apart to give you access to the cavity" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/9.jpg" caption="Carefully cut around the anus - be mindful of where the intestines are." %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/10.jpg" caption="You should be left with something that looks like this - a bum hole attached only to the intestines!" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/11.jpg" caption="Reach inside the cavity and scoop around the inside of the ribs to free any membranes. Reach as far up to the neck as you can then cup your hand a bit and drag as much of the insides back and out with you as you remove your hand (sorry I don't know how best to describe this!)" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/12.jpg" caption="You should get out something looking like this!" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/13.jpg" caption="The lungs are usually stuck to the ribs. This are bright red and bubbly- use your fingers to free them and remove. The cavity should look clean and blood free when you have finished." %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/14.jpg" caption="This green thing is the gall bladder - smells foul when burst and can make the meat tast bitter so try to keep in tact! If you want giblets locate the heart and liver and put to one side before disgarding the rest" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/15.jpg" caption="Heart (left), liver (right)" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/16.jpg" caption="Check the neck cavity. This should be clear and meat visible, remove any excess fat or air sac and tubes that were not pulled through with the rest of the inners" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/17.jpg" caption="Air sac looks like this" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/18.jpg" caption="Chop the excess neck and save to making gravy" %}

{% include image.html src="2020/12/23/19.jpg" caption="Finally turn the bird over and remove the preening gland from the parsons nose (just cut around and remove)." %}
